Help Center

Electricity Bill Guides

Where is my Reference Number?

It is normally printed on the upper part of your electricity bill. Enter the digits without spaces.

Reference Number vs Meter Number

The meter number identifies the physical meter; the bill lookup normally uses the billing reference/account identifier.

How to download a duplicate bill

After opening the official bill page, use its print/download options to save a copy.

Bill not found?

Check the company selection and digits. If the official portal is temporarily unavailable, try again later.
